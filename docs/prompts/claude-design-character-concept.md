# Gemini Guy · 3D character concept sheet (reference for a Blender build)

Design a polished, bold, multicoloured CHARACTER CONCEPT SHEET for "Gemini Guy", the mascot tutor of a study app for college students. A 3D artist will build him in Blender from this sheet, so it must be precise: clean vector art (SVG), flat colour with soft shading, labelled hex colours, and proportions on a unit grid.

## Who he is
A small, joyful, slightly goofy tutor. He teaches inside a bright white-box room with two floating browser windows (notes on the left, a board on the right). Students also play with him (tickle, high-five, catch, snacks, dance parties) to level him up.

## Body plan (keep this, refine the details)
- Body = head: a white gumdrop (sphere radius 0.8 units, flattened, slightly flared base). Matte white #f7f7f5 with a soft sheen.
- Face on the body: big glossy black eyes (#131316) with two white highlights each, thin dark brows (#2c2224), a small smile, an open mouth with a pink tongue (#e48c88) for talking, and pink cheek blush.
- An indigo-to-violet graduation cap (#3d5ad6 to #6a4fd0), with a velvet board, a gold button and a gold tassel.
- NO arms or legs. Two floating mitten hands with thumb nubs and two small floating rounded feet (Rayman style), all white #fdfdfc.
- Total height is about 2.6 units, floor to cap top. Hands float at about body height, 1.0 unit out from the centre. Feet float just above the floor.

## Sheet contents (one artboard each)
1. **Turnaround**: front, 3/4, side and back, on a 0.1-unit grid with height and width callouts. Show where each piece floats (hands, feet, cap gap).
2. **Shape language and materials**: callouts for every material with hex, roughness and gloss (matte body, glossy eyes, velvet cap, metallic gold button and tassel), plus how the cheeks and highlights sit on the surface.
3. **Expression sheet**: neutral, happy (eyes become ∩∩ arcs), talking (open mouth), thinking (one brow up, eyes up and to the side), surprised (big eyes, small "o" mouth), sleepy (closed ∪ eyes) and proud/level-up.
4. **Key poses** (2 to 4 keys each, with arrows for motion and squash/stretch): idle bob, wave, point left, point right, think, listen, shrug, hop, celebrate (spin), giggle (tickled), high five, catch, yum (snack).
5. **Dance moves** to encourage the student, each as 4 key poses with beat counts: "Bounce" (8-count groove with hand claps), "Spin" (wind-up, 360, pose), "Robot" (stiff popping hands, head ticks), plus 2 new dance ideas of your own.
6. **Level-up cosmetics**: level 1 to 6 variants (rainbow cap, gold tassel, sparkle trail, party lights, a small accessory each level), shown as small turnarounds with a bold multicolour palette: teal #12a4b4, coral #ff6b5b, sun #ffc83d, violet #7c5cf2, lime #4cc76a, pink #ff6fae.

Style: friendly, premium, readable at small sizes, bold outlines optional. Bold type for the labels. Make it delightful.
